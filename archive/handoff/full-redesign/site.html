Computer Science student at Sabancı University passionate about AI applications in healthcare. Building the future of medical AI.

Driven by the potential of AI to bridge gaps in modern medicine, I focus on developing intelligent systems that are as ethical as they are efficient. My work at Sabancı University centers on collaborating with professors and fellow students to build projects that connect deep CS knowledge with complex medical hurdles to find practical, scalable solutions.

Outside of the lab, I balance the logic of neural networks with painting — a creative outlet that allows me to view technical challenges through a more expressive, lateral-thinking lens.

PHYSICAL REALITY

THE BODY
AS PRACTICE

I train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu as a blue belt. The mat is one of the few places where thinking too much will get you tapped out — you have to react, adapt, and accept when you lose.

Archery came later. It's the opposite of BJJ in almost every way — no opponent, no urgency, just you and the stillness between breaths.

Daily conditioning keeps everything running. Not for aesthetics — for the simple fact that having energy changes how you move through the rest of life.

BJJBLUE BELT
Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. Ground grappling, positional control, submissions. The sport where the smaller person can win if they're smarter.
ARCHERYRECURVE
Focus, breath control, stillness under pressure. The discipline of archery is mostly about learning to do nothing while everything tries to make you flinch.
CONDITIONINGDAILY
Strength and endurance work that supports everything else. Consistency over intensity. The goal is showing up tomorrow.
THE STUDIO

OIL
PAINTING

Painting with oils since I was a teenager. A medium that dries slowly and demands commitment — you can't undo a stroke, you can only paint over it.

Most of my work starts without a clear plan. I mix a color that feels right, make a mark, and respond to it. The discipline is in resisting the urge to over-explain.
